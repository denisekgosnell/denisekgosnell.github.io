Skill + Schedule: Donna, Full Time

“If you were ever lucky enough to have me, you wouldn’t want to share.”

Donna Paulsen, Suits
Goal

Turn your desk manual into a skill Donna carries anywhere, then give her the evening shift: briefing written, calendar booked, 4:54 PM email.

She never sends. Ever.

Every reply and every handoff note lands in your Drafts folder and waits for you. She never deletes anything either. Both rules are written into the skill itself.

The steps

  1. Have Donna write her own playbook

    Inside your Donna project, paste:

    The playbook 
    Turn this project’s triage instructions into a reusable skill file
called donna-replies, modeled on the template at
https://denisekgosnell.github.io/Donna/skills/donna-replies/SKILL.md
Fill in every placeholder with what you already know from this project:
my VIPs, my delegates, my #1 priority, my voice.
Then give me the finished SKILL.md as a file I can download.
    You should see Claude hands back a file card named SKILL.md. Watch it work: it’s writing your playbook from everything you taught it in Case File 02.

  2. Download the file

    Click the file card and choose Download.

    You should see SKILL.md in your Downloads folder. That file is yours: your rules, your people, your voice, portable anywhere Claude goes.

  3. Hand it to Cowork

    Skills and scheduled tasks live in Cowork, Claude’s workspace for jobs that run without you. Open Cowork from the top of the Claude desktop app, then:

    • Open Skills.
    • Choose Add skill (on some screens it’s an Upload button).
    • Pick the SKILL.md you just downloaded.

    Menus move around in young software. If Skills isn’t where we said, paste this into Cowork: Where do I add a skill file here? Walk me through it based on what’s on my screen. Or wave Denise over.

    You should see donna-replies listed among your Cowork skills. She has her playbook. Now we put her on the clock.

  4. Give her the evening shift

    In Cowork, open Scheduled tasks → New task, and paste:

    The standing order 
    Every weekday at 4:30 PM, use my donna-replies skill:
1. Triage today’s inbox and save the red replies and yellow handoffs
   to my Drafts.
2. Write my Daily Briefing as a document titled "Daily Briefing: [today’s
   date]" with four sections:
   • the 5 things in my inbox that need ME (one line + recommended action)
   • the replies you drafted today
   • what you ignored and why, in one short paragraph
   • my first meeting tomorrow
3. Save it to my Google Drive in a folder called "Donna Briefings".
4. Create a calendar event today, 4:55 to 5:00 PM, called "📋 Briefing
   with Donna", with the document link in the description and an EMAIL
   reminder 1 minute before the event.
Also: do a dry run for today RIGHT NOW so I can check your work.

    Scheduled tasks not where you expect? It moved areas recently. Ask Claude: Where do I create a scheduled task in Cowork?

    You should see The task saved to your scheduled list, and the dry run already working in front of you.

  5. Watch the dry run land

    Give her a minute or two, then check all three, in order:

    • Google Drive has a Donna Briefings folder with today’s doc inside.
    • Today’s calendar shows 📋 Briefing with Donna at 4:55, doc link in the description.
    • Your Gmail Drafts hold the replies and handoffs from today’s triage.
    You should see All three, without touching your keyboard again. And at 4:54 PM today, the email notification arrives with your briefing link inside.
The trick

Claude never emails you. Your calendar does. The briefing lands at 4:54 PM from a sender you already trust. No new app to check, no new noise: one appointment, every day, with everything that matters attached.

On Outlook? Two notes for the evening shift

In the standing order, swap Google Drive for OneDrive and drop the word EMAIL from the reminder line. Outlook’s calendar API only lets apps set pop-up reminders, so your 4:54 nudge arrives as a calendar notification on laptop and phone, briefing link one tap away.

Want a true 4:54 email in Outlook too? Open any Briefing event in Outlook on the web → Edit → More options → Remind me → + Add email reminder. Or grab Denise after the workshop for the five-minute Power Automate version that does it forever.

Advanced: Donna runs both calendars

Juggling a work Microsoft 365 calendar, a personal Google one, and a board calendar or two? Connect both calendar connectors, then add a morning scheduled task: Donna reads every calendar, folds the unified day, conflicts included, into your briefing, and, if you like, writes “busy” holds onto your primary calendar mirroring the others.

The insight: no calendar ever needs access to another. Donna is the only one who sees them all, and she writes into one.

If step 1 balks: two fallbacks

Plan B: use the ready-made template. Download donna-replies.zip, unzip it, and upload the SKILL.md inside via Cowork → Skills → Add skill. It still has {{PLACEHOLDER}} blanks, so first ask Claude: Interview me for each placeholder in this file, then give me the finished SKILL.md to download.

Plan C: for the rare exec who lives in a terminal (Claude Code):

  1. git clone https://github.com/denisekgosnell/denisekgosnell.github.io.git && cp -r denisekgosnell.github.io/Donna/skills/donna-replies ~/.claude/skills/
You did it when…

The 4:54 PM email shows up with your briefing link.

Why this mattersYou didn’t just use an assistant today. You hired one, and you own her playbook: one file, written in plain English, that works wherever you take it.
